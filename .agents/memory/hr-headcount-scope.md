---
name: HR headcount scope
description: Product decision governing SubHub daily attendance records.
---

HR & Attendance records exactly one total present headcount per SubHub per day. Do not reintroduce employee-level attendance entry into the active workflow.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose one daily total rather than individual employee attendance.

**How to apply:** Extend the daily total and its save history instead of adding employee rosters, shifts, or individual statuses to the active HR entry flow.
